GROCERY GAUNTLET

Survive the Shift!

Clean up spills, sweep glass, kill pests, and restock shelves before customers arrive!

HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

WASD - Move

SHIFT - Sprint (run faster)

MOUSE - Look around

CLICK - Use current tool

1 - Mop (clean spills)

2 - Broom (sweep glass)

3 - Spray (kill pests)

E - Restock empty shelves

ESC - Pause menu

U - Unstock all shelves (debug)

TASKS

Spills - Red puddles, use MOP to clean

Glass - Shiny shards, use BROOM to sweep

Pests - Rats & roaches, use SPRAY to kill

Empty Shelves - Use E to restock

MINIMAP

Red Circles - Pest spawn zones

Gray Boxes - Stocked shelves

Dark Box + Red Dot - Needs restock

Green Arrow - You
